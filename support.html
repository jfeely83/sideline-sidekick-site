Sideline SideKick Tracker

Support

Sideline SideKick Tracker is an iOS app for high school football coaching staffs. Coaches log plays from the sideline during a game and get live stats as the game goes on, plus a postgame report when it’s over.

A staff sets up their team once — roster, playbook, and team colors — and then everyone signed in to that team can log plays together. The app keeps working when the connection on the field drops and syncs back up when it returns.

Need help?

Email jfeely@ssvotech.org with questions, bug reports, feature requests, or to ask for your account to be deleted. Please include the version of the app you’re running and, if you’re reporting a problem, what you were doing when it happened.

Replies usually come within a few days.
