Sideline Headset

A wireless headset for your staff, on the phones you already carry.

Headset puts your coaching staff on one line. Your voice goes straight from your phone to the other coaches' phones, encrypted the whole way, with no server in the middle and nothing recorded — because there is no recording to keep, to hand over, or to lose.

Headset is included with a Sideline SideKick Tracker coach subscription. There is nothing extra to buy. The coach who subscribes creates the team; every coach on that staff can use the line, whether or not they were the one who paid.

It is a second app and it needs a SideKick team. You sign in with the same account — there is no separate sign-up — and you have to be staff. Parents and players who joined with an invite code do not get the line, which is the same boundary the playbook sits behind.

What it does

One button, for a thumb that is not looking

The talk button is the only control that matters, and it is sized for somebody watching the field rather than the phone. Everything else on the screen is deliberately smaller than it is.

Breakouts, made of people

A coordinator who needs his two position coaches pulls those coaches off the main line — you choose people, not a room you then have to persuade everyone to join. When it is done, they are back on the line.

A board, so nobody else has to work the app

Dispatch shows every coach, which channel they are on, and moves them with one tap. It exists so that a comms tech can run the staff's headsets and no coach has to think about the app at all during a game.

Signal you can read at a glance

Each coach carries three bars and a colour. The numbers underneath are round-trip time and packet loss, and neither is a thing anybody on a sideline is going to act on — so they are not shown.

On your wrist

There is an Apple Watch app, so a coach can hear the line and see who is on it without taking a phone out.

When the stadium has no signal

This is the case it is built for. When a stadium's service is unusable, your staff joins one coach's Personal Hotspot. Every phone is then on the same network and the app connects them directly over it — no carrier, no internet, no server.

NFHS 1-6-2 allows coaches and non-players to use electronic devices; it never allows communicating with a player during live play. Headset is coach-to-coach, like the Booth in SideKick. Check your own association's rules — they govern your Friday, not this page.

What it stores

Almost nothing, and no student information of any kind. What it keeps is a short list of who is on the line right now: your name, which channel you are on, and whether you are muted. No ads, no analytics, nothing sold, nothing used to track you.

Your voice is never recorded. It is carried directly between phones and encrypted the whole way; no server is in the middle of the call.

Where it is up to

Headset is early, and it is fair to say so. It is a prototype for a staff to try alongside the equipment they already trust, not a replacement for it. Take it onto the field as a second option first, and tell us what breaks — Support has the address.

Getting it

Headset is a separate download from Sideline SideKick Tracker, and it is not on the App Store yet. When it lands it is included with your coach subscription — there is no second thing to buy. If you want it as soon as it is out, email us and we will tell you the day it ships.
