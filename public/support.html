Sideline SideKick Tracker

Support

Questions, bug reports, feature requests, account deletion.

Sideline SideKick Tracker is an iOS app for high school football coaching staffs. Coaches log plays from the sideline during a game and get live stats as the game goes on, plus a postgame report when it's over.

A staff sets up their team once: roster, playbook, and team colors. After that, everyone signed in to that team can log plays together. The app keeps working when the connection on the field drops and syncs back up when it returns.

Need help?

Email support@sideline-sidekick.com with questions, bug reports, feature requests, or to ask for your account to be deleted. Please include the version of the app you're running and, if you're reporting a problem, what you were doing when it happened.

Replies usually come within a few days.

Plenty of the common ones are already answered on the coach's FAQ: cost, devices, what happens with no signal, fixing a play you logged wrong, and who can see your roster.

Sideline Headset

Sideline Headset is a second app from the same developer: a wireless headset that lets a coaching staff talk to each other during a game over their own phones. It is in testing and is not yet on the App Store.

You sign in to it with your existing Sideline SideKick account, and it puts you straight on your own staff — there is no separate sign-up and no invite code. It is for coaching staff only; a parent or player following a team in the read-only Fan role cannot go on the line.

Support is the same address as everything else on this page. Two things worth including when you write in about Headset: whether the coach who had trouble was on wifi or cellular at the time, and whether the staff was sharing one phone's hotspot. Nearly every connection problem is answered by those two facts.

Headset stores different information from the tracker and records no audio at all, so it has its own privacy policy.
