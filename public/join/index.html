Install the free app What will I see?

Free for parents, players and fans. The app is on iPhone and iPad.

Already have the app?

Tap the link again and it will open straight to the join screen with your code filled in. If it opens this page instead, open the app yourself, go to Team → Join a team with a code, and enter the six characters above.

What you get

  • The live score and the play-by-play while the game is going on.
  • Season stats for the whole team, and for your player.
  • The schedule and the result of every game.

You join as a fan. Fans never see the staff's formations, play names or game plan — that side of the app is for coaches. The parents' page shows exactly what a fan sees.
