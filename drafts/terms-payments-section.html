6. Fees, subscriptions and refunds

Joining a team with an invite code is free, and always will be. Assistant coaches, parents and players do not pay anything to use the app.

Creating a team requires a coach subscription. It is sold through the App Store as an auto-renewing subscription at the price shown in the app at the time you buy. One subscription covers your account rather than a single team, and it allows you to create and run up to five teams. If you need more than five, contact us at Support and we will sort it out — there is no second subscription to buy.

Where an introductory free period is offered, it is granted by Apple and is limited to one per Apple ID. If you have already used an introductory offer for this app, you will be charged when you subscribe.

Renewal and cancellation. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before it ends. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings; we cannot cancel it for you, and deleting the app does not cancel it.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Purchases are made through the App Store, so refund requests go to Apple and are decided under their policies. If Apple refunds a purchase, the subscription ends immediately rather than at the end of the period.

If your subscription lapses, your data stays. Nothing is deleted because a subscription ended. You keep access to the seasons, rosters and games already recorded, and you can export them. What a lapsed subscription stops is creating new teams.

Prices may change. A change will not affect a period you have already paid for, and Apple will tell you before a renewal at a new price.
